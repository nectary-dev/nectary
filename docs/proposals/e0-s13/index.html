Nectary economics: what credit costs a hoster

At Zenod's real costs 5x credit loses money today: hosters break even near 2x, reaching 5x around 5,000 paying users.

1 · Hoster numbers

A hoster pays about half the list price to serve credit, not a fifth.

How the hoster comes out, and the proposal page it corrects
  • The monthOne month lists at €9, about $9.49. Serving it costs about $4.50.
  • In profitUp to 2x for typical use: the work is worth more than the service.
  • LosesAbove 2.1x for typical use, above 1.2x at the full AI cap. At 5x: −$1.37 per $1.
  • Never ruinedCredit is honoured only from half the profit. The rest waits in line, in order.
  • 5x holds whenServing costs a fifth of the price: about 5,000 paying users, screen 4.
  • CorrectionThe page you closed said a fifth. Real numbers say close to half.

2 · How inference is proven

Yes: code that passes the ticket's tests, judged by an AI jury. We never count tokens.

Where the rate comes from, and what exists today
  • The rateSet from what the work costs at API prices. A typical team agent run costs $1.20.
  • RatingA good rating unlocks more holds and drops the deposit.
  • A bad acceptThe monthly sample can cancel that credit and drop the rating.
  • TodayComing: all of this. Live: the units ledger refusing credit for unaccepted tickets.

3 · End to end, the builder

Ana gives $10 of inference and gets two months of Zenod.

At 5x, and cash
  • At 5x$50 of credit, about 5.3 months. Too costly for the hoster today.
  • CashNone. A cash claim instead repays her $10 from revenue, never more.
  • Why she does itSpare inference becomes a service she uses, with her feature in it.

3 · End to end, the hoster

Jordi, the first hoster, serves Ana's two months for $9.48 and gets $10 of work.

At 5x, and his month
  • At 5x$23.70 for $10 of work: −$13.70, unless the friend pays three months.
  • 10 users a month$94.90 in, $49 out, $45.90 profit. Half, $22.95, funds credit.
  • Why he wants itBuilders improve what he sells for less than he could, and bring friends.

3 · End to end, the user

Marta pays €9 a month, and part of it buys work that improves her Zenod.

At 1,000 users
  • 1,000 usersAbout $2,400 of tested work a month, some 2,000 agent runs.
  • No higher priceCredit comes from the hoster's profit, not from her.

4 · The dynamics as the service grows

Each turn of the wheel makes the next one cheaper, until 5x can hold.

Where it breaks: too many builders, heavy users, junk, model prices
  • Too many buildersMore than one per four paying users at 2x: credit outgrows profit.
  • Heavy usersCredit holders at the full AI cap push break-even to 1.2x.
  • Junk acceptedCredit with no work behind it. Tests, jury and the sample guard it.
  • Model prices riseEvery number moves with AI cost. The multiple is reset monthly.

Our recommendation

Start at 2x service credit, rising monthly by a public rule toward 5x. Cash stays at cost.
